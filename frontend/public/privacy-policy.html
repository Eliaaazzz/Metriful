Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 23, 2026

1. Information We Collect

  • Account information from Apple or Google sign-in.
  • App usage information, nutrition logs, and fitness activity you provide.
  • Technical data needed to operate, secure, and improve the service.
  • If you connect Apple Health, the activity, sleep and weight readings you permit Metriful to read.

2. How We Use Information

We use data to authenticate your account, deliver core product features, improve recommendations, maintain reliability, and provide customer support.

3. Data Sharing

We do not sell personal information. We may share limited data with infrastructure providers strictly for service operation, security, and compliance.

When you use AI Coach, your message, recent conversation, logged nutrition totals and saved goals are sent to Google's Gemini service to generate a response. Apple Health summaries are included only when you enable the separate sharing switch in Coach. This choice is off by default. Changing it clears the current conversation. We do not send your account name or email as part of Coach context. Data submitted to Gemini is processed under the applicable Google service terms.

4. Data Retention

We retain data for as long as needed to provide the service and meet legal obligations. You may request account and data deletion through support.

Apple Health summaries and Coach conversations are held in app memory for the current screen visit. This integration does not save them in Metriful's database. Leaving the screen clears them. Our Coach telemetry records run identifiers, tool names and timing without prompt text or health measurements. You can revoke Metriful's data access in the Health app at any time.

5. Security

We apply technical and organizational safeguards, but no internet transmission or storage method is guaranteed to be 100% secure.

6. Contact

Privacy requests: support@aurafitness.org
