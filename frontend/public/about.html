About Aura Fitness

We build and operate our own software platform.

Aura Fitness is a digital-native software company based in Melbourne, Australia. We design, build and run one product: Metriful, an AI meal scanner that turns a photo into an itemized, editable nutrition estimate. No client work, no consulting, no reselling — the product on this site is the company, and the founder below writes its code.

The product

Metriful, at a glance

Everything below is publicly verifiable — the app is live on the App Store and the web app runs at this domain.

What it is
An iOS and web app that turns a meal photo into an itemized nutrition estimate: every detected food with grams, calories, macros and a confidence level. On LiDAR iPhones, depth measures the plate so portions come from geometry, not guesswork. Every line is editable in one tap.iOS + web
Who it's for
People who quit calorie apps because logging took longer than eating — and people managing metabolic health who need logging they'll actually keep doing. The core loop is one photo, one review, one clear next step.consumer health
Stage
In production. Metriful is live on the App Store and on the web at aurafitness.org, with updates shipping regularly — see the release notes.live
How it works
Snap a meal → the vision pipeline identifies each food and estimates portions → uncertain items are flagged for a one-tap review → the day's remaining targets and one concrete suggestion come back. The product page walks through the full flow, including a worked example scan.photo → estimate
Built in-house
One codebase ships the iOS and web clients (React Native + Expo). Our own API (Spring Boot / Java) runs on Google Cloud Run in australia-southeast2, on PostgreSQL with pgvector for food-similarity search, with Gemini vision models and OpenAI embeddings in the scanning pipeline. We build and operate all of it ourselves.own platform
