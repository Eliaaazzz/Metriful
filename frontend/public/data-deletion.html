User Data Deletion

Last updated: February 14, 2026

1. How to Delete Your Account

You can delete your account directly in the app:

  • Open Metriful and go to Profile
  • Tap Settings
  • Tap Delete Account
  • Confirm the deletion when prompted

Your account and all associated data will be permanently removed immediately.

2. What Gets Deleted

  • Account profile and authentication mapping
  • Nutrition logs, meal history, and related metadata
  • Hydration logs, weight logs, and workout tracking records
  • Uploaded meal photos and saved recipes

3. Additional Help

If you are unable to access the app, you can contact us at support@aurafitness.org to request manual deletion. Some minimal records may be retained only when required by law.
